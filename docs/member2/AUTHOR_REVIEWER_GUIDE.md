# Guide for authors and reviewers

This guide explains how notices move from a draft to the public pages.
All times in the system are Korea Standard Time (Asia/Seoul).

## For authors

### Write a notice
1. Log in and open **My Notices**, then **New notice**.
2. Type at least a title and press **Save draft**. You can come back later.
3. Fill in the rest: summary, body, category, department, audience,
   visibility, publishing office and the requested publication start.
   - **Visibility PUBLIC**: anyone can see it. **CAMPUS**: only signed-in users.
     Within CAMPUS, audience All permits every signed-in account; Students,
     Faculty or Staff permits only accounts in that administrator-verified group.
     PUBLIC notices remain public regardless of their audience label.
   - **Action deadline** is for things like "apply by 10 December". It is shown
     to readers but does not hide the notice.
4. Optional: add an **event** (date, time, venue, organizer) and **attachments**
   (PDF, PNG, JPG or DOCX, up to 4 MB through the shared website).

### Preview and submit
- **Preview** shows the notice exactly as students will see it, with a
  "PREVIEW - NOT PUBLISHED" banner. Opening it changes nothing.
- **Submit for review** checks every required field. If something is missing,
  each problem is listed next to its field.

### After review
- **Returned**: read the reviewer's reason in My Notices, edit, and submit again.
- **Rejected**: final. Read the reason; create a new notice if needed.
- **Scheduled / Published**: the reviewer chose the publication time.
- To fix a published notice, use **Create correction**. Readers keep seeing
  the current version until a reviewer approves your correction. Then an
  "Updated" date appears. Only one correction can wait at a time.
- You may **withdraw** your own published notice. You must give a reason.

### If someone else saved first
If you see "This notice has been modified by another user", your text is
still in the form. Choose **Load latest version** to compare side by side, or
**Copy my changes** to keep a copy. Nothing is saved until you decide.

## For reviewers

1. Open **Review Queue**. The oldest items are first. "Correction" rows show a
   field-by-field comparison with the public version.
2. You cannot decide on a notice or correction that you wrote. Another
   reviewer must do it.
3. **Approve** opens the publication settings:
   - **Publish now**, or **Schedule** with a future start time.
   - Optional end time. The notice disappears at that exact minute.
   - Priority (Normal, High, Urgent) and **Pinned** (at most 5 pinned at once).
4. **Return** sends it back to the author with your reason (at least 10 characters).
5. **Reject** is final and needs a reason.
6. On an approved notice you can change priority and pinning, **withdraw** it
   (with a reason), and **archive** it after it has expired or been withdrawn.
7. For a linked event you can **postpone** (new date and reason; the old time
   stays in its history) or **cancel** (reason; the event stays visible as
   cancelled).

Every decision is recorded in the audit log with your name, the time and the reason.
