# Visual assets

The portal mark and six professor avatars are original SVG illustrations for this project. The mark is a portal identity, not an official university seal. Avatars represent fictional sample profiles.

Campus photography is an AI-generated illustration of a fictional university, not a photograph of Kyungdong University. The frontend labels it as illustrative.

Files: frontend/public/assets/kdu-mark.svg, campus-detail.svg, campus-hero.webp (1600px), campus-hero-small.webp (800px), avatars/professor-1.svg through professor-6.svg. The WebP copies are optimized from the original built-in image generation output.

Generation mode: built-in image_gen.

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: university information portal hero photograph. Primary request: create a polished realistic editorial photograph of a fictional modern Korean university campus, an elegant red brick and pale stone library with broad glass windows, leafy green trees and a wide pedestrian courtyard, softly forested mountains in the distance, a few tiny anonymous students seen from behind in the distance, natural late spring morning light. Composition: wide 16:9 landscape architectural view, welcoming campus building framed center-right with quiet trees and sky at left for a navy web layout crop. Colors: natural green foliage, warm brick, neutral limestone, blue sky. Real camera texture and realistic architecture, understated academic atmosphere. No identifiable real campus, no readable signs, no text, no logos, no watermark, no exaggerated fantasy architecture. This image will be labelled as illustrative.
